## Why this milestone gates everything downstream

H1 is a claim about a **calibrated** uncertainty channel. If the forecaster's intervals were miscalibrated, `ppo_uq` would be consuming a channel that misstates dispersion, and a null result could not be separated from a broken input. Protocol section 4.5 therefore makes this a blocking gate: RL training may not consume forecasts unless the quantile model beats seasonal naive on pinball loss *and* its calibrated 80% interval covers within the pre-registered band.

## The uncertainty channel is calibrated out of sample, not just in sample

The interval-width multiplier (1.155) is fitted on **validation only**, moving 80% coverage from 0.7416 to 0.8017 against a target of 0.8. Coverage on validation is therefore in-sample with respect to that one scalar, and reporting it alone would overstate the case.

On the untouched **test** split the same multiplier gives PICP80 = 0.8162 and expected calibration error 0.0099 — better calibrated than validation (ECE 0.0174), on data the calibration never saw.

**Coverage is never reported without width.** An interval reaches any coverage by widening; the 80% interval here is 27.4% of the mean actual value on test, so it is informative rather than vacuously wide. Winkler score penalises width and misses jointly and is reported for the same reason.

## What the sigma channel actually is

`sigma` is derived from the calibrated interval by the frozen definition `(q90 - q10) / (2 * 1.2816)`, and validation check 4b confirms the stored column matches that formula to zero absolute deviation. It is a **dispersion estimate under a normal reference**, not a claim that demand is normally distributed. The RL arms consume it as an ordinal signal of forecast uncertainty; nothing downstream assumes normality.

Quantile crossing before the monotone repair ran at 8.48% of adjacent quantile pairs. Independent quantile regressions cross; the repair sorts each row's quantiles, and the pre-repair rate is recorded in the manifest so the repair is visible rather than hidden. After repair the stored forecasts contain no crossings and no negative sigma.

## What this milestone does not establish

- **That the forecaster is good enough for operational use.** The gate asks whether the uncertainty channel is trustworthy enough to test a hypothesis about, not whether a transit operator should deploy it.
- **That calibration holds under the surge and disruption scenarios.** Those are simulator constructions, not held-out data. Coverage is established on the historical test split only; the placebo arm exists precisely because a well-calibrated channel can still be useless to a policy.
- **Anything about H1.** A calibrated forecaster is a precondition for the experiment, not evidence for its hypothesis.
- **Out-of-regime transfer.** The pre-pandemic period is registered separately as EXP-ROBUST-004 (decision D-11) and is not part of this fit.
