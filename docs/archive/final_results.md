## Research question

Does giving a reinforcement-learning train-induction controller a *calibrated forecast uncertainty* signal improve real-time metro scheduling, relative to the same controller given only a point forecast?

The question is causal about the **information**, not about the architecture, so the design holds architecture, parameter count, observation dimensionality, reward, training budget, seeds and evaluation days fixed across arms, and varies only what the uncertainty channel carries.

## Hypotheses

| id | hypothesis | family |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | The uncertainty-aware arm lowers `left_behind_fraction` under `surge`. | confirmatory (primary) |
| H1c | The uncertainty-aware arm beats a **placebo** arm carrying a shuffled sigma of identical marginal distribution. | confirmatory (co-primary) |
| H2 | The advantage holds across all four scenario families. | exploratory |
| H3 | Multi-objective training beats single-objective (wait-only) training. | exploratory |
| H7 | The availability channel carries decision-relevant information. | exploratory |

The placebo arm is the reason this design can separate *information* from *dimensionality*: it has the same observation size and the same marginal sigma distribution, with the association to the true forecast error destroyed. Any effect present in both the uncertainty and placebo arms is an artefact of the extra input, not of the uncertainty.

## H1 verdict

### H1 = NOT SUPPORTED

Because the confirmatory test rejects the null in the direction opposite to H1: `ppo_uq` leaves *more* passengers behind than `ppo_point` by 0.013742 (95% BCa CI [0.006570, 0.018523], excluding zero and lying entirely above the SESOI of 0.0020), at Holm-adjusted p = 0.00774114. The conclusion rests on that interval and on the robustness checks -- a distribution-free cross-check and a leave-one-seed-out jackknife that remains significant for every replicate -- not on any post-hoc power argument.

The placebo arm sharpens the reading. The uncertainty arm is worse than the point arm, **and also worse than the placebo arm** that carries an uninformative sigma of the same marginal distribution. So the degradation is not explained by the extra observation dimensions alone: under this reward and training budget, the calibrated sigma was worse than noise with the same shape. That is a stronger negative than a simple null, and it is reported here as the experiment produced it.

The uncertainty arm spends less energy and less cost, and leaves more passengers behind with longer waits. That is the signature of **under-dispatching**. Decisively, it also attains a worse `total_reward` — the single scalar objective both arms were trained to maximise — so this is not a defensible position on a trade-off frontier. Both arms optimise the same scalarisation; one simply optimises it less well.

Whether that is a property of the information or of the optimiser cannot be separated at this training budget, and the limitations below say so.

## Limitations

1. **Underpowered for the SESOI.** Detecting the registered SESOI of 0.0020 would need n = 69; the study ran n = 10, giving a minimum detectable effect of 0.005909. The primary result is conclusive only because the observed effect is far larger than that floor. A *null* result at this sample size would not have licensed a claim of equivalence.
2. **One untuned PPO configuration.** Amendment A-05 froze a single configuration for every arm. This protects the comparison from selective tuning, but it means the uncertainty arm's failure could be an optimisation or sample-efficiency effect at 60k timesteps rather than a property of the information. The design cannot separate these.
3. **Effort asymmetry between arms and baselines.** The MILP received four corrective amendments; the RL arms received none. This runs against the proposed method.
4. **`left_behind_fraction` is a partial welfare measure.** It counts distinct passengers who could not board, not how long they then waited or whether they abandoned the journey. Two policies with identical left-behind fractions can differ in realised harm.
5. **Simulator quantities largely unvalidated externally**, as tabulated above.
6. **Synthetic OD model** (assumption A03) drives the primary endpoint; the OD sensitivity ablation exists precisely because of this.
7. **Regime screen fell back.** The pre-registered screen found no qualifying run of at least 365 days; the configured start date was used instead. Conclusions inherit whatever regime instability remains.
8. **Single line, single operator, single data source.** No claim of external validity beyond the studied network.

## Conclusion

**H1 = NOT SUPPORTED.** Under this reward, training budget and network, supplying the controller with a calibrated forecast-uncertainty signal did not improve real-time induction; it produced measurably worse passenger service than the point-forecast controller, and worse than a placebo signal of identical shape. The result is reported as the experiment produced it. It constrains the claim that uncertainty-awareness is automatically beneficial in this setting; it does not establish that no uncertainty-aware method could help, and the limitations above bound how far it generalises.
