# Manuscript Corrections Required Before / During Implementation

Per research brief §36: where implementation reveals that a manuscript claim is too strong or a
design is unsound, the correct response is to **document it and propose a defensible correction**,
not to change the experiment so the claim becomes true.

Severity: **BLOCKING** = implementation cannot proceed correctly without resolving it ·
**SUBMISSION** = must be fixed before the paper is submitted · **ADVISORY** = should be fixed.

| ID | Severity | Manuscript location | Problem | Proposed correction | Needs author confirmation? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **MC-06** | **BLOCKING (before M5)** | §5, p. 6: *"synthetic passenger arrivals generated as Poisson processes whose rate is set by the demand-forecast model plus noise"* | **Circular design.** If the simulator's ground-truth arrival rate is produced by the forecasting model, the forecaster is correct by construction, forecast error equals a noise term we chose, and the predictive dispersion σ becomes an artefact of our own parameter. The central hypothesis (H1) would then be untestable — the experiment would measure our noise setting, not uncertainty-awareness. | Ground truth is generated by a demand process **fitted to observed historical ridership**; the forecaster sees only observable history and produces genuine, uncontrolled error. Specified in `experimental_protocol.md` §5.1 and guarded by `tests/test_no_circular_demand.py`. | **YES — materially changes the experiment's validity.** |
| **MC-01** | SUBMISSION | Abstract p. 1; §2 p. 3 | *"To our knowledge, this is the first proposal to jointly address demand-forecast uncertainty, multi-objective optimization, and decision explainability for real-time train induction."* An unverifiable universal negative, and conjunction novelty is the weakest form of novelty claim in this literature. The manuscript's own Table 1 shows each ingredient already exists in transit RL. | Rescope to a **measurement** claim: we isolate, under a placebo control, the contribution of explicit forecast-uncertainty representation in a metro induction policy — a question the cited work does not answer. | **YES — changes the stated contribution.** |

| **MC-09** | BLOCKING (before M11) | §6, p. 6: *"two-tailed t-tests, or Wilcoxon rank-sum where normality does not hold"* | Three problems. ## Confirmation status

**All five blocking items were confirmed on 2026-08-26** by the project owner, on the auditor's
recommendation. Full reasoning and consequences: [`docs/decisions.md`](decisions.md).

| Item | Decision | Record |
|---|---|---|
| **MC-06** | Accepted. Ground-truth demand is fitted to observed historical ridership; the forecaster sees only observable history. The manuscript's §5 generative design is not implemented. | D-01 |
| **MC-01** | Accepted. Novelty rescoped from a universal-negative conjunction claim to a placebo-controlled *measurement* claim. | D-09 |
| **MC-05** | Accepted. `milp_rolling` added alongside `milp_static`, registered in advance as `EXP-BASE-005`. Accepted consequence: this may shrink or erase the RL advantage, and that result will be reported. | D-03 |
| **MC-08** | Accepted, **option (a)**. Reframed as an availability-information ablation. No train-health prediction performance is reported as a research result. MetroPT-3 grounding is an optional off-critical-path extension. | D-04 |
| **MC-09** | Accepted. Paired tests, training seed as the unit of analysis, Holm on a two-test confirmatory family, BH on exploratory, TOST equivalence on a null. | D-05 |

These decisions were taken **before** any dataset was downloaded, any model trained or any experiment
run, so they form part of the pre-registration rather than amendments to it;
`docs/experimental_protocol.md` §12 remains empty.

The advisory items (MC-02, MC-03, MC-04, MC-07, MC-10 – MC-14, MC-16 – MC-18) and the
research-integrity item **MC-15 (reference verification)** remain the author's to apply to the
manuscript text itself; they are outside this codebase.
