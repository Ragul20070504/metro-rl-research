## What fairness here does and does not mean

- It means every method faced the same network, simulator, scenarios, evaluation seeds, stochastic realisations, horizon, fleet constraint, endpoint and reward scales.
- It means no method read a channel outside its registered information set, and none read the future.
- It means tuning happened on seed pools disjoint from every pool the comparison is scored on.
- It does **not** mean the baselines are the best possible versions of themselves. Two of them were corrected after their first results looked implausible (amendments A-10 and A-11), and the corrections strengthened them. A third correction might exist that has not been found. The asymmetry that remains is one of *effort*: the MILP surrogate has been revised three times and the heuristic's grid extended once, while the RL arms run one untuned frozen configuration (A-05).
